What are carboxylic acids?
Carboxylic acids

compounds containing the functional group –COOH

Made from

Key facts

Vinegar

An aqueous solution containing ethanoic acid (acetic acid).

Position

–COOH is always on the end carbon: no number needed.

Names

End in -oic acid.

2Acid properties
Weak acids

pH 3–5

Depends on concentration.

Litmus

Turns blue litmus red.

Slower

React more slowly than HCl of same concentration.

Reaction with metals
Acid + metal

salt + hydrogen

Magnesium

Mg + 2CH₃COOH → (CH₃COO)₂Mg + H₂. Fizzing; colourless solution of magnesium ethanoate.

Zinc

Fizzes more slowly than with hydrochloric acid. Sodium is dangerous; metals below hydrogen do not react.

Formulae of ethanoates

Ionic

Magnesium ethanoate: Mg²⁺ and ethanoate ions, CH₃COO⁻.

Balancing

Two CH₃COO⁻ balance one Mg²⁺: (CH₃COO)₂Mg. Zinc: (CH₃COO)₂Zn.

Exam tipThe metal replaces the H of –COOH: CH₃COOH → CH₃COONa.
Reaction with carbonates
Acid + carbonate

salt + carbon dioxide + water

Sodium carbonate

Na₂CO₃ + 2CH₃COOH → 2CH₃COONa + CO₂ + H₂O

Calcium carbonate

2CH₃COOH + CaCO₃ → (CH₃COO)₂Ca + CO₂ + H₂O

✓ Say this

–COOH: C=O and separate O–H. CH₃COOH is ethanoic acid. Salts are ethanoates. Weak means partly ionised.

✗ Avoid this

Weak means dilute; ethanoic acid called methanoic; chlorides from ethanoic acid.
