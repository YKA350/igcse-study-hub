The alkali metals

Lithium, sodium and potassium are soft, reactive metals that react with water in the same way and get more reactive down Group 1.

Sealed tubes

Explaining the trend

Why reactivity rises.

Chemistry only
Chemistry only

Bigger atoms, weaker attraction

More shells

Atoms get bigger down the group.

Further away

Outer electron is further from the nucleus.

Easier to lose

Weaker attraction, so Na → Na⁺ + e⁻ is easier.

Don't lose marks

Avoid this

Oxide in water; oxygen gas; reactivity falling down Group 1; 'more shells' alone.
