Redox

Why it is redox

Oxidation

Bromide ions lose electrons: oxidised.

Reduction

Chlorine gains electrons: reduced to Cl⁻.

Oxidising agent

takes electrons from another species: Cl₂ here

Exam tipBr₂ + 2I⁻ → 2Br⁻ + I₂: bromine is the oxidising agent.

Exam technique

Don't lose marks

Say this

Group 7 less reactive down, Group 1 more. Halogen is the element; halide is the ion. Name colour change and halogen formed.

Avoid this

Mixing up the group trends; halogen vs halide; saying bromine is a gas.

Quick check

Test yourself

1. State of bromine at room temperature?Liquid, red-brown
2. What does chlorine do to KBr?Displaces bromine: orange
3. Which halogen is the oxidising agent?The one gaining electrons
