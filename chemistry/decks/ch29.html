Repeat units and monomers

Both directions.

Method

Monomer to repeat unit

  1. Draw the alkene with C=C in the middle and four groups above and below.
  2. Change the double bond to single.
  3. Draw a continuation bond from each carbon, through square brackets.
  4. Write n after the bracket. Change nothing else.
Spec 4.49C understand how to write the structural and displayed formula of a polyester, showing the repeat unit,…
Method

Polymer to monomer

  1. Take two neighbouring chain carbons: that is the repeat unit.
  2. Remove brackets, n and continuation bonds.
  3. Put the double bond back between the two carbons.

Disposing of addition polymers

A problem.

Problems

The problems

Inert

Strong covalent bonds: unreactive.

Not biodegradable

Bacteria cannot break them down.

Burning

Toxic gases: CO, HCl (from PVC); also CO₂.

Exam technique

Don't lose marks

Say this

Repeat unit has single C–C. Addition gives one product; condensation also water. Brackets with continuation bonds. Inert and non-biodegradable.

Avoid this

Double bond in repeat unit; missing n; no continuation bonds.
