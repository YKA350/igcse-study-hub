Alkanes

Alkanes are the simplest hydrocarbons: single bonds only. They burn well but do little else.

A homologous series

Trends and patterns.

Series

A homologous series has

  1. The same functional group.
  2. Similar chemical properties.
  3. A trend in physical properties.
  4. The same general formula.
  5. Members differing by –CH₂–.
Exam tipSay: similar chemical properties because they have the same functional group.

5

Substitution with halogens

UV light.

Substitution

Substitution reactions

Conditions

Halogen plus alkane in UV light, e.g. sunlight.

What happens

One hydrogen replaced by a halogen: mono-substitution.

Methane + bromine

CH₄ + Br₂ → CH₃Br + HBr. The orange colour fades.

Ethane + chlorine

CH₃CH₃ + Cl₂ → CH₃CH₂Cl + HCl (a gas, not hydrochloric acid).

Exam technique

Don't lose marks

Say this

General formula CₙH₂ₙ₊₂. Saturated: only single bonds. Halogens react in UV light by substitution, giving HBr or HCl too.

Avoid this

Forgetting UV light; forgetting the hydrogen halide; calling substitution addition.
