Reversible reactions and equilibria

Some reactions go backwards as well as forwards. In a sealed container they settle into a balance called dynamic equilibrium.

Dynamic equilibrium

Sealed container.

Chemistry only
Sealed

Why sealed?

Sealed container

Nothing added, nothing escapes (heat can).

Example

CaCO₃(s) ⇌ CaO(s) + CO₂(g). Open container: CO₂ escapes, no reverse reaction.

Result

A reversible reaction in a sealed container reaches equilibrium.

Predicting shifts

  1. More pressure: 2 → 1 molecule, shifts right, paler (more N₂O₄).
  2. Lower temperature: shifts exothermic (forward), colour fades.
  3. Higher temperature: shifts endothermic (reverse), darkens.

Exam technique

Don't lose marks

Say this

Rates equal, concentrations constant. Shift to the side with fewer gas molecules. Catalyst: no change in position.

Avoid this

Concentrations equal; reactions stop at equilibrium; pressure shifts when moles equal.
