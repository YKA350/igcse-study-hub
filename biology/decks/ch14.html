Sampling with quadrats

Estimating populations.

Required practical

Estimating population size

  1. Two tape measures at right angles.
  2. Random coordinates (avoids bias).
  3. Count the species in each quadrat.
  4. Repeat 10–20 times; find the mean.
Population

mean per quadrat × (total area ÷ quadrat area)

Spec 4.4B practical: investigate the distribution of organisms in their habitats and measure biodiversity using quadrats
Calculation

Worked example

Daisies in a field
Givenmean 6 per 0.25 m² quadrat; field 500 m²
Scale500 ÷ 0.25 = 2000 quadrats
Multiply6 × 2000
12 000 daisies

Factors

How factors affect populations

Abiotic

More light, water and minerals: more plants. Temperature, pH and O₂ limit where organisms live.

Biotic

More food: population rises. More predators, disease or competition: falls.

Energy flow

Pyramids and the 10 % rule.

Pyramids

Ecological pyramids

Numbers

Can be any shape: one tree feeds thousands of insects.

Biomass

Dry mass of living material at each level.

Energy

Always a true pyramid: energy is lost at each level.

Spec 4.6 understand the names given to different trophic levels, including producers, primary, secondary and…
Energy

Only about 10 % is passed on

Lost as

Heat from respiration (movement, warmth), faeces, urine, parts not eaten (bones, fur).

Kept

A small amount becomes new body tissue (biomass).

Efficiency

energy transferred to next level ÷ energy at previous level × 100

Exam tipThis is why food chains are short: too little energy is left for another level.

The carbon cycle

Removes CO₂

Photosynthesis only.

Returns CO₂

Respiration (plants, animals, decomposers) and combustion.

RememberFossil fuels form from dead matter over millions of years.

Nitrogen cycle

The nitrogen cycle (Biology only)

BacteriaWhat they do
DecomposersBreak down proteins and urea to ammonium
NitrifyingAmmonium → nitrite → nitrate (need oxygen)
Nitrogen-fixingN₂ gas → nitrogen compounds; in root nodules of legumes
DenitrifyingNitrates → N₂ gas in waterlogged soil: reduces fertility
