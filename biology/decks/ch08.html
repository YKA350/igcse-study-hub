What is homeostasis?

Negative feedback

A change from normal is detected and a response reverses it.

Controlled

Temperature

Too hot and too cold

Too hot

Vasodilation: arterioles widen, more blood near the surface, more heat radiated. Sweating: evaporation takes heat. Hairs lie flat.

Too cold

Vasoconstriction: arterioles narrow, less heat lost. Shivering: respiration releases heat. Little sweating. Hairs stand up, trapping air.

Common mistakeCapillaries do not move. It is the arterioles supplying them that widen or narrow.

The nephron

1. Ultrafiltration

High pressure forces small molecules into the capsule. Cells and proteins stay.

All glucose goes back by active transport, plus some ions and water.

3. Water reabsorption

Loop of Henle and collecting duct, controlled by ADH.
