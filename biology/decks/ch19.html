Definition

What is evolution?

Evolution

gradual change in the inherited characteristics of a population over many generations, which can lead to new species

Populations

Individuals do not evolve: populations do.

No need

Organisms do not change because they need to.

Source

Species and evidence

Isolation, extinction, fossils.

Species

New species and extinction

New species

Populations separated by sea or mountains face different conditions. Selection favours different alleles until they cannot interbreed to give fertile offspring.

Extinction

Environment changes faster than the species can adapt: new predator, disease, climate change, habitat loss.

Species

organisms that interbreed to give fertile offspring

Fossils

Fossil evidence

Fossils

Preserved remains or traces in rock. Deeper is older.
