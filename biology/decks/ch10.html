Required practical

Testing a leaf for starch

Boil in water
→
Warm in ethanol (water bath)
→
Rinse in water
→
Add iodine

Why

Boiling kills and stops reactions; ethanol removes chlorophyll; rinsing softens; blue-black means starch.

Common mistakeNever heat ethanol over a flame. It is flammable.

De-starch

Keep the plant in the dark for 48 h so starch is used up.

Light

Chlorophyll

Variegated leaf: only green parts make starch.

Required practical

Net gas exchange with indicator

Bright light

Photosynthesis > respiration: CO₂ taken up, indicator turns purple.

Dim light

Rates equal (compensation point): stays red.

Dark

Only respiration: CO₂ released, indicator turns yellow.

RememberPlants respire all the time, day and night. Tube with no leaf is the control.
