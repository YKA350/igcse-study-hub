1Selective breeding
Steps of selective breeding
Selective breeding

humans choose parents with desired traits and breed them over many generations so the trait becomes stronger

Desired traits in crops

Yield

More grain, larger fruit.

Resistance

Disease, drought, frost, pests.

Quality

Taste, colour, shelf life, even ripening.

Exam tipDwarf wheat puts energy into grain and does not fall over in wind.
One wild cabbage, many vegetables

Wheat

High-yield crossed with disease-resistant; select offspring with both over many generations.

Exam tipEach vegetable came from selecting a different feature.

Gain over generations

Dogs

All breeds were bred from wolves by artificial selection.

Pattern

Repeated selection steadily strengthens the trait.

Problems with selective breeding

Inbreeding

Related parents raise the chance of two harmful recessive alleles: genetic disorders.

Less variation

Smaller gene pool: one new disease could wipe out the herd or crop.

Welfare

Broiler chickens' legs fail; high-yield cows get mastitis.

Slow

Many generations; unwanted alleles may come too.

2Cloning plants
Runners and cuttings

Runners (natural)

Strawberry sends out a stem; new plant roots at the node. Mitosis makes identical copies.

Cuttings (artificial)

Cut a shoot, dip in rooting hormone, plant in compost: grows into a clone of the parent.

Tissue culture

Advantages

Thousands of identical plants fast, in small space, all year; disease-free; saves rare species.

Disadvantages

No variation: all share one weakness. Costly, sterile lab.

3Cloning mammals
Cloning by nuclear transfer

Who is the clone of?

The nucleus donor, sheep A: not the egg donor or surrogate.

Diploid nucleus

From a mature body cell: 2n restores the full set.

Transgenic animals

Use

Animals modified to make human proteins in their milk, e.g. clotting factors. Cloning copies the valuable animal.

Concerns

Low success rate; clones may age early; less variation; welfare and ethics.

✓ Say this

Select parents with the trait; breed; select best offspring; repeat over many generations. Nucleus from body cell into enucleated egg; embryo into surrogate.

✗ Avoid this

Selective breeding changes genes directly; Dolly is the surrogate's clone; clones vary.
