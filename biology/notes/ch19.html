1Darwin
Charles Darwin

HMS Beagle

Darwin studied wildlife, including Galápagos finches with beaks suited to different foods.

1859

On the Origin of Species proposed natural selection. Wallace reached the idea independently.

What is evolution?
Evolution

gradual change in the inherited characteristics of a population over many generations, which can lead to new species

Populations

Individuals do not evolve: populations do.

No need

Organisms do not change because they need to.

Source

Variation already exists, from random mutation.

2How selection works
The steps
Key points

Acts on phenotypes

But changes allele frequency in the population.

Repeats

Every generation, over many generations.

Exam tipWrite: variation, advantage, survive, reproduce, pass on alleles, more frequent.
Common mistakeNever say 'passes on genes' without 'reproduces'.
3Examples
Antibiotic resistance

Why quick

Bacteria divide about every 20 minutes, so resistance appears in months.

The problem

MRSA is hard to treat; easy cures may become deadly.

Slowing resistance

Prescribe less

No antibiotics for viruses or minor illness.

Finish the course

Kills the remaining bacteria.

Farming and research

Limit farm use; develop new antibiotics.

The peppered moth

Industrial era

Soot darkened trees; camouflaged dark moths survived and became common.

Clean air laws

Pale moths became common again.

Exam tipBirds are the selection pressure.
4Species and evidence
New species and extinction

New species

Populations separated by sea or mountains face different conditions. Selection favours different alleles until they cannot interbreed to give fertile offspring.

Extinction

Environment changes faster than the species can adapt: new predator, disease, climate change, habitat loss.

Species

organisms that interbreed to give fertile offspring

Fossil evidence

Fossils

Preserved remains or traces in rock. Deeper is older.

Horse

Larger over time, toes reduced to one hoof.

Incomplete

Most organisms decayed before fossilising.

Other evidence

DNA comparison

More similar DNA suggests a closer relationship.
