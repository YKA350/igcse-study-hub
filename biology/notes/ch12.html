1Tropisms
Growing towards or away
Tropism

A growth response to a directional stimulus.

Positive

Growth towards the stimulus: shoots towards light, roots down towards gravity.

Negative

Growth away: shoots up away from gravity.

Exam tipTropisms help shoots reach light and roots anchor the plant and reach water and minerals.

2Auxin
Auxin is made in the tip

Made in tips

Diffuses down behind the tip.

Shoots / roots

Shoots: elongation. Roots: inhibited.

How a shoot bends to light
  1. Light from one side.
  2. Auxin moves to the shaded side.
  3. Shaded cells elongate more.
  4. Shoot bends towards the light.
Common mistakeLight does not destroy auxin. It moves to the shaded side.
Shoots and roots on their side

Shoot

Auxin collects on the lower side and stimulates elongation: lower side grows faster, shoot bends up (negative geotropism).

Root

Auxin collects on the lower side but inhibits elongation: upper side grows faster, root bends down (positive geotropism).

The clinostat

Rotating

Gravity acts equally on all sides: seedlings grow straight out horizontally.

Stationary control

Roots bend down and shoots up.

Exam tipKeep in the dark so light does not affect the result.
RememberAgar with auxin on one side of a cut stump makes it bend away from that side.
Uses of plant hormones

Weedkillers

Synthetic auxins make broad-leaved weeds grow too fast and die. Cereals and grass are unaffected: selective.

Rooting powder

Auxin on cuttings makes roots grow quickly: many clones.

Fruit

Ethene ripens fruit. Hormones can make seedless fruit without fertilisation.
