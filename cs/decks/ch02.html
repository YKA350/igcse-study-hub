Why wording matters (1/2)

Construct

A smaller part from which something is built. Algorithms are built from sequence, selection and iteration.

Sequence Step-by-step instructions in the correct order.

Selection A choice is made between alternatives, e.g. IF the water is at 100 °C then turn off the kettle.

Iteration A process is repeated until a condition is met. It is often called a loop.

Why wording matters (2/2)

A human understands "wait for the water to boil" and keeps checking.

A computer would just wait forever.

The algorithm must say how to tell the water is boiling, e.g. temperature = 100 °C.

It must also say how much water to pour.

Selection and iteration in flowcharts

Representing them

Selection is a question (diamond) with two alternatives, YES and NO.

Iteration is a question where the NO arrow goes back and repeats the question until the answer is YES.

Example

check temperature of water = 100 °C. If NO, check again. If YES, turn off kettle and pour water.
