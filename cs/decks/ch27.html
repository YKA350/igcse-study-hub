Professionalism

The skill and competence expected of a person in a professional setting.

Computer scientists write software, create mobile apps, design embedded systems, devise security policies and invent products.

They work for big companies such as Google and Apple, start-ups, hospitals, universities, or are self-employed.

Codes of conduct

Codes of conduct

The British Computer Society (BCS) is the Chartered Institute for IT. Its Code of Conduct sets out the standards members must uphold.

It says to respect the wellbeing, privacy and security of the environment and people, and never stop learning and gaining professional knowledge.

Many computer scientists pay for membership of a professional association: access to conferences, training and publications, networking, and a CV that carries weight with employers.
