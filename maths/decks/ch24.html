24.3

Inverse functions

Inverse

The inverse undoes the function

Find the inverse of f(x) = 3x − 4
yy = 3x − 4
Swapx = 3y − 4
Rearrangey = (x + 4)/3
f⁻¹(x) = (x + 4)/3
  1. Write y = f(x).
  2. Swap x and y.
  3. Rearrange to make y the subject.
Exam tipCheck: f(f⁻¹(x)) = x.

Composite functions

Composite

One function after another

f(x) = 2x, g(x) = x + 3. Find fg(x)
g firstx + 3
Then f2(x + 3)
2x + 6
  1. fg(x) means do g first, then f.
  2. Replace x in f with g(x).
  3. Simplify.
Exam tipfg and gf are usually different.
