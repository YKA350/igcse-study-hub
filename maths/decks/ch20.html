Theoretical and experimental probability

Idea

Probability is a number from 0 to 1

Theory

P = favourable outcomes ÷ all equally likely outcomes.

Experiment

P ≈ times it happened ÷ number of trials.

Scale

0 impossible, 1 certain.

A bag has 3 red and 5 blue. P(red)?
Favourable3
Total8

20.2

Mutually exclusive outcomes

Idea

Can't both happen

Meaning

Two outcomes that cannot happen together.

OR rule

P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B).

Total

All probabilities add up to 1.

P(red) = 0.3, P(blue) = 0.5. P(red or blue)?
Add0.3 + 0.5
0.8

Independent events

AND rule

Independent events: multiply

P(heads) = ½ on each of two flips. P(two heads)?
Multiply½ × ½
¼
  1. Check that one event does not affect the other.
  2. P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B).
  3. Keep fractions or decimals.
Exam tipAND means multiply, OR means add.

Probability given something
