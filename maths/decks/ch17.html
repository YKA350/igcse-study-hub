Tangents, chords and circles

Rules

Tangents and chords

Tangent

A line touching the circle at one point. It meets the radius at 90°.

Two tangents

From one outside point they are equal in length.

Chord

A line from the centre to a chord's midpoint is perpendicular to it.

Angles at the centre and edge

Centre

The angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference.

Same segment

Angles from the same arc are equal.

Semicircle

The angle in a semicircle is 90°.

Intersecting chords

Chords

Intersecting chords

Chord parts 3 and 8 cross another with parts 4 and x. Find x
Product3 × 8 = 24
Other4 × x = 24
x = 6
  1. Two chords cross inside a circle.
  2. Multiply the two parts of one chord.
  3. This equals the product of the parts of the other.
Exam tipOutside the circle: same idea with tangents (tangent² = product).
