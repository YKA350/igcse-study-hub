perpendicular bisector
  1. Perpendicular bisector: arcs of the same radius from both ends, cross above and below, join.
  2. Angle bisector: arc across both arms, then arcs from those points, cross, join to the corner.
  3. Keep construction arcs on the page.
Exam tipNever rub out the arcs.
