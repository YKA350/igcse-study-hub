Solving problems involving the mean

Missing value

Use the mean to find a missing value

The mean of 4 numbers is 7. Three are 5, 8 and 6. Find the fourth
Total7 × 4 = 28
Known5 + 8 + 6 = 19
9
  1. Total = mean × number of values.
  2. Subtract the known values.
  3. The answer is what is missing.
Exam tipTotal is the key.

19.3

Histograms

Histogram

Frequency density

Class 10–20 has frequency 30. Find the frequency density
Width20 − 10 = 10
Density30 ÷ 10
3
  1. Frequency density = frequency ÷ class width.
  2. Bar height is the frequency density.
  3. Frequency = frequency density × class width (the area).
Exam tipThe area of the bar is the frequency.

Quartiles

Find Q1, median, Q3

Data: 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 12. Find IQR
Median7
Q14
Q310
IQR10 − 4
6
  1. Put the data in order.
  2. Median is the middle value.
  3. Q1 is the middle of the lower half; Q3 of the upper half.
  4. IQR = Q3 − Q1.
Exam tipIQR measures spread.
