Section 16.1 The sine ratio
Sine

Use sine to find a side

Angle 30°, hypotenuse 10. Find the opposite side
SOHsin 30° = opp ÷ 10
Rearrangeopp = 10 × sin 30°
5
  1. Label opposite, adjacent and hypotenuse from the angle.
  2. Choose the ratio that uses your two sides.
  3. Write the equation and rearrange.
Exam tipMultiply by the hypotenuse.

The tangent ratio

Tangent

Tangent uses the two shorter sides

Angle 45°, adjacent 7. Opposite side?
TOAtan 45° = opp ÷ 7
Rearrangeopp = 7 × 1
7
  1. Label the sides.
  2. tan θ = opposite ÷ adjacent.
  3. Rearrange.
Exam tiptan 45° = 1.
