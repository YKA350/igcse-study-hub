Problems leading to quadratic equations

Problem

Words to a quadratic

A rectangle's length is 3 more than its width, area 40. Find the width
Equationx(x + 3) = 40
Rearrangex² + 3x − 40 = 0
Factorise(x + 8)(x − 5) = 0
5 (not −8)
  1. Let a letter be the unknown.
  2. Write an equation from the facts.
  3. Solve and reject answers that don't make sense.
Exam tipA length cannot be negative.
