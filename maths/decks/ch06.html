Origin

(0, 0), where the axes cross.

Midpoint

Average the x's and average the y's.

6.5

Parallel and perpendicular lines

Idea

Same slope, or flipped slope

Parallel

Same gradient: y = 3x + 1 and y = 3x − 4.

Perpendicular

Gradients multiply to −1: flip and change sign.

Example

Gradient 2 → perpendicular gradient −1/2.

Line parallel to y = 3x + 1 through (0, 5)
Same gradientm = 3
Interceptc = 5
y = 3x + 5
