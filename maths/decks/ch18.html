Section 18.2 Venn diagrams
Fill

Fill a Venn diagram

30 students: 18 like football, 12 like tennis, 5 like both. How many like neither?
Football only18 − 5 = 13
Tennis only12 − 5 = 7
In circles13 + 5 + 7 = 25
Neither30 − 25
5
  1. Put the overlap (in both) in first.
  2. Subtract it from each set to get the 'only' parts.
  3. The rest go outside the circles.
Exam tipTotal = only A + only B + both + neither.
