Expressions, equations and identities

Idea

Four kinds of algebra

Expression

No equals sign: 3x + 5.

Equation

Has = and can be solved: 3x + 5 = 20.

Formula

A rule linking quantities: A = lw.

Identity

Always true: 2(x + 1) ≡ 2x + 2.

5.2

Simple equations

Balance

Solve by balancing

Solve 4x − 7 = 21
Add 74x = 28
Divide by 4x = 7
x = 7
  1. Think of scales: do the same to both sides.
  2. Undo + or − first.
  3. Then undo × or ÷.
  4. Check by substituting back.
Exam tipx² = 25 gives x = 5 or −5.

Equations and brackets

Brackets

Expand first

Solve 3(x − 2) = 2(x + 5)
Expand3x − 6 = 2x + 10
Subtract 2xx − 6 = 10
Add 6x = 16
x = 16
  1. Expand the brackets.
  2. Collect terms and solve as normal.
Exam tipA minus before a bracket changes every sign.

Fractions

Clear the fractions

Solve x/3 + x/4 = 7
LCM12
Multiply all by 124x + 3x = 84
Solve7x = 84
x = 12
  1. Find the LCM of all the denominators.
  2. Multiply every term by it.
  3. Solve the simpler equation.
Exam tipMultiply every term, including the number on the right.
