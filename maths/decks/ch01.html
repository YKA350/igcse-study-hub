Upper and lower bounds

Idea

How big or small could it really be?

Meaning

A length given as 8 cm to the nearest cm could be anything from 7.5 to just under 8.5.

Rule

Lower bound = value − half the rounding unit. Upper bound = value + half the unit.

Notation

7.5 ≤ length < 8.5

A mass is 60 kg to the nearest 10 kg
Rounding unit10 kg, half is 5
Lower60 − 5 = 55
Upper60 + 5 = 65
55 ≤ m < 65

  2. Biggest answer: use upper bounds for + and ×; for − and ÷ use upper then lower.
  3. Smallest answer: the opposite choice.
  4. Write both answers clearly.
