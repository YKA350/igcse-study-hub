Circumference and area of a circle

Idea

Distance round and space inside

Circumference

C = πd or C = 2πr.

Area

A = πr² (square the radius first).

Radius

Half the diameter.

Circle with radius 5 cm: circumference and area
C2 × π × 5 = 31.4
Aπ × 5² = 78.5
C = 31.4 cm, A = 78.5 cm²

A sector is a fraction of a circle

  2. Arc length = fraction × 2πr.
  3. Sector area = fraction × πr².

Surface area and volume of a cylinder

Cylinder

Cylinder formulae

Volume of a cylinder r = 3, h = 10
Base areaπ × 3² = 28.27
× height28.27 × 10
282.7 cm³
  1. Volume = πr²h (base circle × height).
  2. Curved surface = 2πrh.
  3. Total surface = 2πrh + 2πr².
Exam tipTotal surface area: don't forget both circles.
