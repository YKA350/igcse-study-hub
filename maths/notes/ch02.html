Ratios and percentages

Chapter in a nutshell

  • Ratio: add parts, find one part, multiply

  • Percentage change = change ÷ original × 100

1Working with ratios
What a ratio says

Meaning

3 : 2 means 3 parts for one, 2 parts for the other.

Simplify

Divide both sides by the same number: 6 : 4 = 3 : 2.

Total parts

Add the parts to find how many equal shares there are.

Share $50 in the ratio 3 : 2
Total parts3 + 2 = 5
One part50 ÷ 5 = 10
Shares3 × 10 = 30 and 2 × 10 = 20
$30 and $20
Ratio problems: find one part
Blue : red paint is 4 : 3. There are 12 litres of blue. How much red?
Blue = 4 parts12 ÷ 4 = 3 litres per part
Red = 3 parts3 × 3 = 9
9 litres
  1. Add the ratio numbers to get the total parts.
  2. Divide the amount by the total to get one part.
  3. Multiply one part by each ratio number.
  4. Check the shares add back to the total.
Exam tipUse the part you are given first.
2Simple percentages
Percent means out of 100

Fraction

35% = 35/100 = 7/20.

Decimal

35% = 0.35. Divide by 100.

Of an amount

Find 10% (÷ 10), then build up.

Find 35% of 80
10%80 ÷ 10 = 8
30%3 × 8 = 24
5%half of 10% = 4
24 + 4 = 28
3Percentage increase and decrease
The multiplier method
Increase $240 by 15%
Multiplier100% + 15% = 115% = 1.15
Multiply240 × 1.15
$276
  1. Increase: 100% + the percentage, written as a decimal.
  2. Decrease: 100% − the percentage, as a decimal.
  3. Multiply the original by the multiplier.
Exam tipIncrease 3% → 1.03. Decrease 3% → 0.97.
Find the percentage change
A price goes from $80 to $92. Percentage increase?
Change92 − 80 = 12
÷ original12 ÷ 80 = 0.15
× 10015%
15%
  1. Find the change: new − old.
  2. Divide by the original amount.
  3. Multiply by 100.
Exam tipAlways divide by the original.
4Reverse percentage problems
Undo a percentage by dividing
After a 20% discount a jacket costs $64. Original price?
Multiplier100% − 20% = 0.8
Divide64 ÷ 0.8
$80
  1. Write the multiplier for the change.
  2. The new value = original × multiplier.
  3. Divide the new value by the multiplier.
Exam tipDo not take 20% of 64 and add it back.
5Compound interest
Interest on interest
$500 at 4% compound interest for 3 years
Multiplier1.04
Power1.04³ = 1.124864
Multiply500 × 1.124864
$562.43
  1. Find the yearly multiplier (5% → 1.05).
  2. Raise it to the power of the number of years.
  3. Multiply by the starting amount.
Exam tipCompound means the power button.

✓ Say this

Use a multiplier for any percentage change. Divide by the original to find percentage change.

✗ Avoid this

Taking 20% of the new value to undo a discount.
