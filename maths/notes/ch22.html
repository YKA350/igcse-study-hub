1Solving quadratics by factorising

2Completing the square
Complete the square
Write x² + 8x + 5 in the form (x + a)² + b
Half of 84
Square16
Form(x + 4)² − 16 + 5
(x + 4)² − 11
  1. Halve the x number: that goes in the bracket.
  2. Subtract its square.
  3. x² + 6x + 1 = (x + 3)² − 8.
Exam tipVertex is at (−4, −11).
3Solving quadratics by the formula
The quadratic formula
Solve x² + 3x − 2 = 0
a, b, c1, 3, −2
b² − 4ac9 + 8 = 17
x(−3 ± √17) ÷ 2
x = 0.56 or −3.56
  1. Write a, b and c (with signs).
  2. Substitute into x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ 2a.
  3. Work out both answers.
Exam tip'3 s.f.' hints at the formula.
4Problems leading to quadratic equations
Words to a quadratic
A rectangle's length is 3 more than its width, area 40. Find the width
Equationx(x + 3) = 40
Rearrangex² + 3x − 40 = 0
Factorise(x + 8)(x − 5) = 0
5 (not −8)
  1. Let a letter be the unknown.
  2. Write an equation from the facts.
  3. Solve and reject answers that don't make sense.
Exam tipA length cannot be negative.
