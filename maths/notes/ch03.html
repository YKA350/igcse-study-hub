4Negative powers
Negative means flip

Rule

x⁻ⁿ = 1 ÷ xⁿ. The minus does not make it negative.

Example

3⁻² = 1/3² = 1/9.

Fractions

(2/3)⁻² = (3/2)² = 9/4.

5The laws of indices
Three laws, one idea
Simplify (x⁴ × x⁵) ÷ x³
Multiply: addx⁴ × x⁵ = x⁹
Divide: subtractx⁹ ÷ x³ = x⁶
x⁶
  1. Same base multiplied: add the indices.
  2. Same base divided: subtract the indices.
  3. Power of a power: multiply the indices.
Exam tip(x²)³ = x⁶, not x⁵.
