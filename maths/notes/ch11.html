4Areas and perimeters of simple shapes
Area covers, perimeter goes around

Rectangle

Area = length × width.

Triangle

Area = ½ × base × height (height is perpendicular).

Others

Parallelogram: base × height. Trapezium: ½(a + b)h.

Area of a trapezium: parallel sides 6 and 10, height 4

5Surface area and volume
Volume and surface area
Volume of a prism: triangle base 6, height 4, length 10
Cross-section½ × 6 × 4 = 12
× length12 × 10
120 cm³
  1. Cuboid volume = l × w × h.
  2. Prism volume = cross-section area × length.
  3. Surface area = add the area of every face.
Exam tipVolume units are cubed; area units are squared.
