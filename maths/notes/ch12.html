1Circumference and area of a circle
Distance round and space inside

Circumference

C = πd or C = 2πr.

Area

A = πr² (square the radius first).

Radius

Half the diameter.

Circle with radius 5 cm: circumference and area
C2 × π × 5 = 31.4
Aπ × 5² = 78.5
C = 31.4 cm, A = 78.5 cm²
