3Transformations of graphs - translations
Move the graph without changing it

Up/down

y = f(x) + a moves up by a.

Left/right

y = f(x − a) moves right by a.

Careful

The brackets work the opposite way to what you expect.

y = x². Describe y = (x − 3)²
In bracketx − 3
translation 3 right
4Further transformations
Stretch and reflect

Vertical stretch

y = a f(x) stretches by a in y.

Reflection

y = −f(x) flips in the x-axis; y = f(−x) flips in the y-axis.

Horizontal

y = f(ax) squashes by 1/a in x.

y = sin x. Describe y = 2 sin x
Outside fmultiplies y
vertical stretch factor 2
