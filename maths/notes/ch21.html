  1. Write y = kx (or kx² if told).
  2. Put in the known x and y to find k.
  3. Rewrite the formula with k and use it.

Inverse proportion: y = k/x
y is inversely proportional to x. y = 6 when x = 4. Find y when x = 8
k6 × 4 = 24
y24 ÷ 8
