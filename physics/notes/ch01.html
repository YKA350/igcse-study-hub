Working out average speed
Key term · average speed

The total distance travelled, divided by the time taken for the journey.

Example

A car travels 100 km in 2 hours.
Average speed = 100 ÷ 2 = 50 km/h.

average speed = distance moved ÷ time taken
v = s ÷ t
v in m/ss in mt in s
Watch the lettersThe book uses s for distance (not d) and v for speed.
Rearranging v = s ÷ t

Find speed

v = s ÷ t

Find distance

s = v × t

Find time

t = s ÷ v

Exam warningIf a question says “write the equation”, write the real equation, e.g. s = v × t. Drawing only the triangle may not get the mark.
Instantaneous speed
Key term · instantaneous speed

The speed at one particular moment.

  • It is what a car’s speedometer shows.
  • It changes from instant to instant as the driver speeds up or brakes.
RememberAverage speed = the whole trip. Instantaneous speed = right now.
The × 3.6 switch

Speed is usually in metres per second (m/s). You may also see km/h or cm/s. In exams, m means metres, not miles!

Why 3.6?

× 3600 (seconds → hours), then ÷ 1000 (metres → km). 3600 ÷ 1000 = 3.6

Example: 12.8 m/s × 3.6 = 46.1 km/h

A speed trap
  1. Measure 50 m along a straight road (tape or click wheel).
  2. A partner signals: start the stopwatch as the car passes the start.
  3. Stop it as the car passes the finish.
  4. Safety: never measure on the road or pavement. Stay inside the school boundary.
Knows = 50 m, t = 3.9 s
Equationv = s ÷ t
Substitutev = 50 m ÷ 3.9 s
v = 12.8 m/s (= 46.1 km/h)

Reading the shape
Curves and negative gradients

A curve means the speed is changing. For the speed at one instant, draw a tangent and find its gradient.

Sloping down to the right = negative gradient. The displacement from the start is decreasing, so the object is coming back.

How to find a gradient
  1. Draw a big triangle on the straight part of the line.
  2. Choose an easy number for the base (e.g. 2 s, 5 s).
  3. Gradient = rise ÷ run (vertical change ÷ horizontal change).
  4. Read values off the axes, with units – not the grid squares.
Herespeed = 20 m ÷ 4 s = 5 m/s

Same speed, changing velocity

Round a bend at a steady 50 km/h: the speed is constant, but the velocity changes because the direction changes.

average velocity = increase in displacement ÷ time taken

On a displacement–time graph, a line sloping down means the displacement is decreasing – the object is heading back.

The GPS walker
B is 3 km north-west of A. The walk takes 45 min. Find the average velocity.
Knows = 3 km NW, t = 45 min = 0.75 h
Substitute3 km ÷ 0.75 h
4 km/h north-west

The walker follows the roads, so the distance walked is more than 3 km. So average speed > average velocity.

4Practical: investigating motion
Toy car on a ramp

Aim: how does the average speed of a toy car depend on the height, h, of the raised end of the ramp?

  1. Clamp the track. Measure h with a metre rule held perpendicular to the bench, always to the same mark (a fiducial mark).
  2. Measure distance AB. Release the car from A each time.
  3. Time A → B with a stop clock at least 3 times; find the average.
  4. Calculate v = AB ÷ average t. Repeat for other heights.
Results, errors and conclusions
Height h / mt₁ / st₂ / st₃ / sAverage t / sAverage v / m/s
…………(t₁ + t₂ + t₃) ÷ 3v = AB ÷ t

Errors

Human reaction time causes timing errors → repeat and average. A result very different from the others is anomalous – ignore it or repeat it.

Safety

Stack and move heavy runways carefully and use them at low level. Put a catch box with bubble wrap at the end for heavy trolleys.

Conclusion

Use your graph. If the best-fit line is straight and passes through (0, 0), the two quantities are directly proportional.

Better ways to time

Light (timing) gates

The clock times while the card blocks the beam. speed = length of card ÷ time. No reaction time errors – more accurate. Two gates show how speed changes.

Photographs

Stroboscope: flashes at regular known intervals in a dark room, camera shutter open. Video: the frame rate gives the time between frames. Both need a measuring scale in the picture.

5Acceleration
What is acceleration?
Key term · acceleration

The rate at which objects change their velocity.

acceleration = change in velocity ÷ time taken
a = (v − u) ÷ t
a in m/s²v, u in m/st in s

m/s ÷ s = m/s² (“metres per second squared”). Acceleration is a vector.

Speeding up and slowing down
A car speeds up steadily from 20 m/s to 30 m/s in 5 s.
Knowu = 20 m/s, v = 30 m/s, t = 5 s
Substitutea = (30 − 20) ÷ 5 = 10 ÷ 5
a = 2 m/s²
An object hits the ground at 40 m/s and stops in 0.02 s.
Knowu = 40 m/s, v = 0, t = 0.02 s
Substitutea = (0 − 40) ÷ 0.02
a = −2000 m/s²
DecelerationSlowing down means v is less than u, so the answer has a negative sign. A negative acceleration is a deceleration (here 2000 m/s²).
Galileo’s rolling ball

Galileo (born 1564) timed with his pulse and a water clock. Bells were placed so they rang at equal time intervals.

Distance in each equal time increased, so the ball sped up at a steady rate: uniform acceleration.

No time given? v² = u² + 2as
v² = u² + 2as
(final speed)² = (initial speed)² + (2 × acceleration × distance moved)
Don’t stop early!v² = 4000 is not the answer. Take the square root.

Use it when the time is not given. This equation is given to you in the exam.

A vaccine pack is dropped from a helicopter hovering 200 m up (a = 10 m/s²). Find its impact speed.
Knowu = 0 (hovering), a = 10 m/s², s = 200 m
Equationv² = u² + 2as
Substitutev² = 0 + (2 × 10 × 200) = 4000
v = √4000 = 63.25 m/s

Hidden clues

  • “from rest”, “dropped”, “hovering”, “stationary” → u = 0
  • “comes to rest”, “stops” → v = 0
  • Thrown up to max height → v = 0 at top, a = −10 m/s²
  • Convert km → m, min → s, km/h → m/s first
Common mistakes

No square root

After v² = u² + 2as, find v = √(v²).

v − u backwards

Always final minus start. Slowing down gives a negative answer.

Wrong units

Acceleration is in m/s², not m/s. Convert km, min, km/h first.

No direction

Velocity and displacement are vectors: say which way.

Mixing graphs

Flat d–t line = stopped. Flat v–t line = constant velocity.

Only a triangle

Write the real equation, e.g. s = v × t.
