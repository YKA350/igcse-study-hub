Conservation and wasted energy

Energy is never made or destroyed – but not all of it ends up where we want.

Conservation of energy

Energy is conserved

Principle of conservation of energy

Energy cannot be created or destroyed – it is only transferred from one store to another.

Wasted energy

Useful and “wasted” energy

Wasted energy

Energy transferred to unwanted stores – usually the thermal store of the surroundings.

  • Often by heating, sound or friction.
  • A hot water tank loses energy heating the tank and the air.
Watch outUnwanted transfers reduce efficiency.

Wasted energy

Is energy “used up”?

No!

Energy is conserved. The total in the Universe stays constant.

But it spreads out

Wasted energy spreads into the surroundings (and radiates into space). It can no longer do useful things.

So…That's why we still have an energy crisis.

Sankey diagrams

How a Sankey diagram works

Key idea

The width of each arrow is proportional to the amount of energy.

  • Wide arrow = lots of energy.
  • Narrow arrow = a little.
  • Useful goes straight on; wasted usually bends away.

Efficiency

How much of the energy we put in does the job we want?

Efficiency

The efficiency equation

efficiency = (useful energy output ÷ total energy output) × 100%
efficiency = useful energy outputtotal energy output × 100%

Total out = total in

Energy is conserved, so the total output equals the input.

No unit

Efficiency is a ratio. Both energies in the same unit.

Fraction or decimal

It can also be written as a decimal – always less than 1.

Worked example · book p.137

A filament bulb

A 60 W bulb uses 60 J every second and is 5% efficient. How much useful light energy does it give each second?
Knowtotal = 60 J, efficiency = 5%
Substitute5% = useful ÷ 60 J × 100%
Rearrangeuseful = 5 × 60 ÷ 100
useful = 3 J each second
The other 57 J heats the surroundings.

Watch out

Common mistakes

✗ Efficiency = 120%

Never over 100%. Useful goes on top.

✗ “Energy gets used up”

It is conserved. Wasted energy just spreads out.

✗ Outputs don't add up

Total width in = total width out.

✗ Efficiency in joules

Efficiency has no unit (a ratio or %).

✗ Unlabelled arrows

Label each arrow: energy type and amount.

✗ Missing outputs

Add all of them: 350 + 10 + 40 = 400 kJ.
