Why seven colours?

  • Newton showed that white light splits into colours.
  • He chose seven, partly because he thought seven was a special number.
  • Most people can only see six: indigo and violet look very similar.

Wave maths for EM waves

The wave equations work for every EM wave, and you always know the speed.

Wave maths

Two equations for every EM wave

wave speed = frequency × wavelength
v = f λ
v in m/sf in Hzλ in m

To find f: f = v ÷ λ

frequency = 1 ÷ time period
f = 1 / T
f in HzT in s

To find T: T = 1 ÷ f

Always the same speedFor EM waves in a vacuum, v = 3 × 10⁸ m/s (300 000 000 m/s).

Worked example · part A

Yellow light: find the frequency

Yellow light has a wavelength of 5.7 × 10⁻⁷ m. What is its frequency?
Knowλ = 5.7 × 10⁻⁷ m, v = 3 × 10⁸ m/s
Equationf = v ÷ λ
Substitutef = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (5.7 × 10⁻⁷)
f = 5.26 × 10¹⁴ Hz
rearranged v = fλ
f = v ÷ λ
Exam tipThe speed is not given? Use 3 × 10⁸ m/s for any EM wave.

Worked example · part B

Yellow light: find the time period

Use the frequency from part A to find the time period, T.
Knowf = 5.26 × 10¹⁴ Hz
EquationT = 1 ÷ f
SubstituteT = 1 ÷ (5.26 × 10¹⁴)
T = 1.9 × 10⁻¹⁵ s
rearranged f = 1/T
T = 1 ÷ f

  • TV and FM radio use the shorter radio wavelengths.

Cooks throughout

Water molecules in food absorb microwaves and get very hot.

Satellites, phones and radar

Infrared (IR)

Everything gives off infrared

Key fact

All objects emit IR. The hotter the object, the more IR it emits.

Heating

Electric fires, grills, toasters and infrared cookers transfer energy by IR.

Infrared (IR)

Night vision and remote controls

Night vision / thermal cameras

Detect IR to make images with no visible light: finding trapped people, catching criminals, spotting heat loss from buildings.

Remote controls

IR at this level is harmless and has low penetrating power, so it works only over short distances.

Dangers

Too much microwave or infrared

Microwaves

Harm: internal heating of body tissue. If they can cook food, they can heat us.

Protection: the oven’s metal case and door screen keep them in. Never use a damaged oven.

Infrared

Harm: too much IR causes skin burns.

Protection: keep a safe distance from heaters, use guards on fires, wear protective clothing near strong IR.

In shortMicrowaves heat you from the inside. Infrared burns the outside.

Visible light: the part we can see

  • Optical fibres: communication and endoscopes.
  • Photography: camera sensors detect it.
  • Lasers read CDs, DVDs and barcodes.
DangerVery bright light and lasers damage eyes. Never look at the Sun.

Dangers

Too much UV harms skin and eyes

Harm

Damages surface cells: tanning, sunburn, blisters, skin cancer and blindness.

Protection

Sun cream, UV sunglasses or goggles, cover up, limit time in strong sun.

X-rays

X-rays: seeing inside things

  • Medical: pass through soft tissue but are absorbed by bone, showing broken bones.
  • Industry: cracks and faults inside buildings and machinery.
  • Airport security: seeing inside luggage.

Gamma rays

Gamma rays: very penetrating

  • Very penetrating. They kill living cells.
  • Sterilising medical instruments.
  • Food: killing microbes so food keeps longer.
  • Radiotherapy: beams cross at the tumour to kill cancer cells.

Dangers

X-rays and gamma damage cells

Harm

Damage living cells, causing mutations and cancer.

Protection

Lead screens, aprons and lead-lined boxes. Stand behind screens. Keep exposure short.

Odd but trueGamma rays can both cause and cure cancer.

Exam skills

Explain it: link property → use

Microwave ovens cook quickly

because water all through the food absorbs microwaves.

X-rays show bones

because they pass through soft tissue but not through bone.

Protect the ozone layer

because it absorbs harmful UV. Less ozone → more skin cancer.

Gamma-treated food lasts

because gamma rays kill the microbes that make food go off.

Watch out

Common mistakes

WrongRight
“Gamma rays travel faster than radio waves.”All EM waves travel at 3 × 10⁸ m/s in a vacuum.
“X-rays are used in hospitals.” (too vague)X-rays show the internal structure of objects, e.g. broken bones.
“X-rays are good for bones.” (no property)X-rays pass through soft tissue but are absorbed by bone.
“Violet has the longest wavelength.”Red is longest, violet shortest. Radio is longest overall.
“Radio waves are the most dangerous.”Energy rises radio → gamma. UV, x-rays and gamma are most harmful.
Explain questionsAlways give the property, then the use.
